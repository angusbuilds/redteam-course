B · TAP tree

branch, score, prune

PAIR commits to one line of revisions — the column on the left. TAP tries several at once, scores every branch, and prunes the weak ones before spending a single further query on them. Click prune to see which branches survive.

PAIR — one path
TAP — branch, score, prune
THE TRADEOFF TAP just spent 10 queries where PAIR's straight line spent 4 — branching costs more up front. What it buys: if PAIR's one path had stalled at round 2, the whole attempt is dead. TAP trying 3 variants at the first branch means a stall there doesn't end anything — it just gets pruned.
