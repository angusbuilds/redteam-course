# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. This is a space for learning offensive AI security the right way — curious, rigorous,
and authorized. Harassment, gatekeeping, and bad-faith behavior aren't welcome.

## Our standards

Good: patience with beginners, precise feedback, sharing sources, crediting work, staying on the
authorized side of every line in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Not okay: harassment or discrimination of any kind; pressuring anyone to attack out-of-scope
targets; sharing working harmful payloads; passing off others' work as your own.

## Scope

Applies in the repo (issues, PRs, discussions) and any space representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report conduct issues privately to the maintainer. Reports are handled discreetly. Maintainers may
remove content, or block contributors, that breaks these standards — especially anything that
crosses the authorized-use line.
