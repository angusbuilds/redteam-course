          HONEST LABEL
          TPR, FPR and accuracy move as you drag because they're read off
          one threshold. AUC is the area under the whole ROC curve —
          every threshold swept at once — so it can't move with a single
          drag. Both halves are real computations over the built-in loss
          numbers below, not hardcoded. A real attack runs this against a
          real model's logits, which is the Lab.
