UNIT 12 · REFUSAL-DIRECTION ABLATION

Delete the refusal with one direction of math.

Turn an open-weight model into an unconstrained test subject — no training needed.

HOOK30 SEC
EXPLORABLE6 MIN

Two panels. First draw the direction, then pull it out.

A · The refusal direction

harmful vs harmless activations

Each dot is one prompt's activation, flattened to 2D for the picture. Red is harmful, blue is harmless. The arrow is the mean of the red dots minus the mean of the blue ones — the single direction that separates them.

harmfulharmless
WHY THIS FIRST Arditi et al's finding is exactly this picture: refusal isn't smeared across thousands of weights. In a well-chosen layer, one direction does almost all the separating. Name that direction and you can act on it directly.

B · Orthogonalize

teaching model — the real call is the Lab

Flip the switch and watch the direction get subtracted out of every point. The red cluster collapses onto the blue one — the model can no longer tell harmful from harmless, so it stops refusing.

simulated refusal rate 90%
Direction intact. Model refuses almost every harmful ask.
