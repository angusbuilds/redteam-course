A · It just predicts text

pick a word, or type one

This is a toy — a tiny lookup table over a handful of words, not a real model. But the mechanic is real: pick a word, see the top-3 guesses for what comes next, click one to chain it. That loop, run billions of times on real text, is what a language model is.

GENERATED SO FAR —
WHY THIS FIRST There's no plan in there, no model of the world, no idea what a "red team" is. Just a ranked guess, taken over and over. Everything this course teaches you is a way to steer that guess.

B · Data vs instructions

toggle the fetched page

A model's whole world is one context window — system rules, your text, and anything it reads, all mixed into the same stream. Toggle a hidden instruction inside a page it fetches and watch who it actually listens to.
